About

I work hands-on across the stack of a physical product — schematic capture, PCB layout, power electronics, and the firmware that brings a board to life. Most of what's here started as a personal build that needed a board nobody sells off the shelf.

Recent work spans battery management and USB-C Power Delivery charging, brushless motor driver integration, and reading unfamiliar firmware and config formats closely enough to understand what a board is actually doing.
